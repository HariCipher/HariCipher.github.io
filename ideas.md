# Chosen Approach — Paper Instrument

## Design Movement

**Neo-modernist editorial design**, informed by Swiss grid discipline, specimen sheets, and independent design-studio portfolios. The interface should read as a rigorous collection of working evidence, not a security-product landing page.

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence over decoration:** Information, project framing, and source-like metadata provide visual interest before effects do.
2. **Deliberate asymmetry:** Broad editorial fields are intersected by narrow labels, status markers, and rules rather than centered card grids.
3. **Tactile precision:** Warm paper surfaces, hairline rules, and small typographic annotations lend material presence without skeuomorphism.
4. **Quiet moments of surprise:** One lime pulse, a rotary/grid motif, and controlled card offsets reward attention without diluting the work.

## Color Philosophy

The site is based on a warm paper ground (**#E9E6E0**) that makes near-black type (**#111111**) read as ink rather than interface chrome. A softer ivory surface differentiates contained objects from the page without introducing glassmorphism. Warm gray rules establish rhythm and hierarchy. **Signal Lime (#C7F052)** is an intentionally scarce operational cue, used for active filters, a status diode, and high-intent interaction moments.

## Layout Paradigm

The page is a **continuous specimen sheet** with a persistent vertical datum at desktop sizes. Sections operate as long editorial spreads: oversized content occupies the broad left field while labels, indexes, and project metadata attach along a thin right rail. On mobile, the rail becomes sequential provenance text above each content group.

## Signature Elements

1. **Registration marks:** Compact black corner marks and numbered section stamps locate content on the page.
2. **The probe disc:** A fine-lined circular coordinate mark in the hero and work shelf area suggests analysis, systems mapping, and measurement.
3. **Active signal:** A small lime indicator and high-contrast focus outlines make selected and interactive states feel instrumental.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interaction behaves like browsing a carefully indexed physical collection. Navigation reveals purpose with restrained underlines and offset movement; project objects lift by a few pixels and expose their archival metadata. Every behavior is keyboard-reachable, and movement never blocks reading.

## Animation

The page uses short (160–280ms) transform and opacity transitions with a crisp cubic-bezier ease-out. Section labels and metadata may make a subtle staggered entrance only when reduced motion is not requested. The probe disc makes a slow, near-imperceptible rotation; no parallax, looping decorative animation, or attention-seeking transitions are used.

## Typography System

**Space Grotesk** provides the authoritative display voice with tightly controlled oversized headlines. **IBM Plex Mono** is used for metadata, technology labels, status, and ordinal navigation. Body copy uses Space Grotesk at a comfortable reading measure. Large display text is compactly tracked; labels remain uppercase with generous mono tracking.

## Brand Essence

**A systems-minded security developer’s working index for engineered tools, investigations, and interface experiments.**

Personality: **Exacting, observant, independent.**

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise, declarative, and slightly editorial. CTAs use specific verbs rather than generic conversion language. Microcopy acts like provenance information.

> “Systems that earn their complexity.”

> “Open the working record.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The identity uses a custom **S/°** monogram: two offset, bracket-like strokes create a compressed “S” around a precise registration dot. It appears as an outlined graphic symbol on the navigation and favicon, with the editable name wordmark set separately in monospace.

## Signature Brand Color

**Instrument Lime — #C7F052.**

## Style Decisions

- The **S/° monogram** and the monospace **SYSTEMS INDEX** wordmark recur in the navigation and final signature, so the identity remains recognizable beyond the opening headline.
- **Instrument Lime** is limited to operational status, active emphasis, and a compact primary contact action. It is never used as a full decorative background.
- Project records make room for diagrams, process traces, decision notes, and evidence without manufacturing results or personal history that the portfolio owner has not supplied.
- The initial system keeps personal identity and links deliberately neutral until the owner provides verified information; exposed setup wording is avoided so the work still reads as an editorial record.
